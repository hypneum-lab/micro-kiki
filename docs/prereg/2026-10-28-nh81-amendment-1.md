# NH-81 amendment 1 (2026-10-28)

Amends `2026-10-25-nh81-joules-per-token.md` (tag `prereg-nh81-20261025`).
Written after the measurement, so it records deviations; it does not
change any threshold, workload, statistic or decision rule.

## A1. Instrument: IOReport GPU rail is the final instrument on macM1

Preregistered: `powermetrics` (PI-run under sudo) primary, IOReport
"Energy Model" as the alternate, verdict labelled "provisional (IOReport)"
until a powermetrics replication.

Amended: the PI decided (2026-10-28) that the macM1 verdict rests on the
GPU rail read through IOReport, without a powermetrics replication:
sudo is not available to the agent, and the OrbStack VM `srv` (production,
never stopped) makes the CPU/package figures of `powermetrics` unreliable.
IOReport reads the same hardware counters for the GPU rail (channel
`GPU Energy`, cumulative, nJ). The independent confirmation remains the
NVML measurement on the KXKM RTX 4090 (section 8 of the prereg).

## A2. CPU energy not available

The IOReport CPU channels (`CPU Energy`, mJ) stayed frozen during the run
(`cpu_j = 0` in every window), so no CPU descriptive is reported. The
preregistration already made CPU/package descriptive only; the primary
metric is unaffected.

The preregistered 20 s rest windows (52, models loaded, nothing running)

sessions' jobs, second-brain workers, OrbStack). Both are reported in
`docs/results/2026-10-25-nh81-joules.md`.
