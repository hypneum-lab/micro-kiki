## Background noise (why the guard did not pass)

**Main finding: on this card, NVML utilisation at idle depends on the
P-state, so the preregistered 10 % threshold is not a usable "GPU at
rest" test here.** After the crash loop was stopped, the card dropped to
P8 (SM 210 MHz, memory 405 MHz, board 21 W) and NVML reported a steady
36-42 % GPU and 30-34 % memory utilisation (20 s trace at 1 s, 20:19),
with only ComfyUI (idle), Xorg and gnome-shell on the GPU (login screen
on `:0`). At 15:29, with the card at P2 (2565 MHz, 67-76 W), the same
resident set read 1-4 %. Utilisation is the fraction of time a kernel is
running, not normalised by clock; a light, constant background load
(display composition and/or ComfyUI) occupies 40 % of the time at the
lowest clocks. Its energy is small (board 20.6 W over the 60 s probe,
P8) and would be subtracted by the rest-window baseline, but the guard
rejects it. The attempt-1 readings (16-21 %) mix this effect with the
crash loop below: between the loop's bursts the 30 s trace at 16:45 read
1-2 %, because each restart kept the clocks up.

Attempt 1, in detail:

Nothing on KXKM was stopped, killed or reconfigured.

* **16:00-19:24, a crash-looping service.** `kxkm-llama-moe.service`
  (user `kxkm`, systemd user unit; `llama-server` with
  Qwen3.5-35B-A3B Q3_K_XL, port 8000, alias `qwen-32b-awq`) restarted
  every ~5 s. Each start briefly appeared as a GPU compute process and
  raised utilisation to 16-43 % for ~2.5 s of every ~5.5 s cycle
  (30 s trace at 0.5 s, 16:45). Idle probe 16:21-16:22 (60 s, no model
  loaded): mean utilisation 9.9 % (max 42 %), board energy 2707 J =
  45.0 W mean, three short-lived foreign compute PIDs besides ComfyUI.
  Guard readings: 16-21 % at every 10-min check from 16:22 to 18:34
  (one 11 %). By 19:25 the loop had slowed (1 start in 30 s).
* **From 18:50, an N10-C freeze job of another session.**
  `experiments.procgen_multiagent_gtm_comm.harness.pretrain` (seeds 101,
  1 M steps, `/ai/saisail/n10c-freeze/`, about 12.8 GB VRAM, board at
  ~170 W) ran from the N10-C venv; guard readings 39-68 % with a busy
  Python process. Under the replication pause rule, a run would have
  paused for it anyway.
* Resident throughout: ComfyUI (pid 712018, 652 MiB, idle), the gpu-swap
  orchestrator (:18890), Xorg/gnome-shell.
  Board idle power between events: 26-67 W (P5 to P2).

## Next step (PI decision needed)

The replication cannot pass the frozen guard on this host without a
change, and changing the guard needs a new preregistration (r3) frozen
before the next attempt. Options, for the PI:

* keep the card out of the low P-states during the measurement window
  (e.g. a session with the display/compositor or ComfyUI idle in a
  defined state), which reconfigures the host and is the owner's call;
* or define "GPU at rest" for KXKM in clock-normalised or power terms
  (for instance: rest-window board power within a fixed band above the
  P8/P2 idle, plus no foreign compute process, which the NVML guard
  already enforces), keeping every other rule.

Once the guard can pass, the run needs about 1-1.5 h (48 pairs plus
calibration). `nh81-measure.sh` refuses to overwrite a `measure.jsonl`,
so move the previous log aside first.
