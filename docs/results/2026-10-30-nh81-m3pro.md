## Result

Primary block (W1 generation, fused spiking path, GPU rail):
**R-hat = 0.670, 95 % CI [0.662, 0.677]**, 12/12 valid pairs,
iso-quality met. Preregistered decision: **withdraw** (R-hat <= 1.1);
the CI lies entirely below 1, i.e. the spiking model uses more energy
per token than its dense twin on this GPU (paired difference spiking -
dense +13.3 mJ/token, CI [13.0, 13.7]). Every secondary block points
the same way.

R = (J/token dense) / (J/token spiking); R < 1 means the spiking model
uses more energy.

| block | pairs | dense mJ/tok | spiking mJ/tok | R-hat M3 Pro [95 % CI] | R-hat macM1 [95 % CI] | decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **W1 gen, fused (primary)** | 12/12 | 27.1 | 40.4 | **0.670 [0.662, 0.677]** | 0.91 [0.83, 1.01] | withdraw |
| W1 gen, event | 12/12 | 26.8 | 69.1 | 0.388 [0.385, 0.392] | 0.48 [0.47, 0.48] | withdraw |
| W2 scoring, fused | 12/12 | 0.242 | 0.450 | 0.539 [0.533, 0.545] | 0.73 [0.73, 0.74] | withdraw |
| W2 scoring, event | 12/12 | 0.244 | 0.681 | 0.359 [0.355, 0.362] | 0.45 [0.45, 0.46] | withdraw |

Sensitivity readings (descriptive, prereg r3 style): gross ratio (no
rest subtraction) and fixed-reference ratio equal R-hat to 3 decimals in
every block, because the rest power (~10 mW) is < 0.1 % of run power.

Throughput (tokens/s, dense vs spiking): W1 fused 458 vs 289, W1 event
458 vs 163, W2 fused 100.7 k vs 33.1 k, W2 event 101.0 k vs 24.8 k.
Mean GPU-rail power during runs: W1 dense 12.4 W vs spiking 11.7 W
(fused); W2 dense 24.4 W vs spiking 14.9 W (fused). As on macM1, the
spiking model draws a little less power but runs much longer (the LIF
loop over T = 6 ticks), so its energy per token is higher. The gap is
larger than on macM1 (R 0.67 vs 0.91 in the primary block), mainly
because the M3 Pro runs the dense generation at a lower power for the
same throughput (12.4 W vs 18.4 W on the M1 Max).

## Limits

One pair of 26.5 M models, one seed, fp32, dense Metal kernels; the
same instrument family as macM1 (IOReport GPU rail), so the M3 Pro adds
hardware diversity, not instrument independence. No A/A run (dense vs
dense) was made to calibrate the pair-to-pair noise; the CIs come from
the bootstrap over 12 pairs. Nothing is said about neuromorphic
hardware or sparse kernels.
